Eight Ages, One Tale of Love

About the dates. The earliest events, such as Creation, the Patriarchs, and the Exodus, cannot be tied to agreed years, so “c.” means “around” and scholars disagree. Dating grows firmer from the age of the kings onward. The Second Coming and the Last Judgment are still to come, and no one knows the hour. Jesus Christ stands at the center and is the fulfillment of the whole story.

    One Tale, One Promise, One Savior

    All of salvation history is a single plan of God, stretching from Creation to Christ and from Christ into eternity. Every covenant prepares the next.

    Adam: life given, and friendship with God
    Noah: God’s vow to keep creation safe
    Abraham: a family that would bless every nation
    Moses: freedom for a people, and the Law
    David: a throne that will never fall
    Jesus Christ: the new covenant that never ends

    The Tale Goes On

    God’s love for mankind runs unbroken from Creation and the covenants, through the Resurrection of Christ and the birth of the Church. As Christians we walk on in faith, waiting for the promise of life everlasting.
